Instrument logos shown next to symbols (`components/ui/InstrumentBadge.tsx`).

- `flags/`: circular country flags from [circle-flags](https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags) v2.7.0, MIT licence (`flags/LICENSE.md`). Currency pairs show the base and quote flags, US indices the US flag.
- `gold.svg`: drawn for this app.

TradingView's own logo files are not copied: they are TradingView's assets and index logos (Nasdaq, S&P, Dow Jones) are trademarks of their owners.
