# AlphaLogFX Privacy Policy

Effective from: 1 November 2026

## 1. Who controls your data

The controller of your personal data is Mikołaj Twardosz (“we”, the “Controller”).

Contact on personal data: contact@alphalogfx.com, technical help: support@alphalogfx.com.

## 2. What data we collect

| Data | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| E-mail address, first name or display name, password (stored only as a hash) | you give it when signing up | creating and running the account, signing in |
| Language, time zone, account currency, app settings | you give or set them in the app | running the app as you chose |
| Trading accounts and trades (instruments, prices, sizes, results, notes, emotions, screenshots, import files) | you enter or import them | keeping the journal, statistics, discipline monitor |
| Subscription data (plan, status, dates, customer identifiers at the payment operator, date of accepting the Terms) | created at purchase | running the subscription and accounting |
| Invitation code, if you use one | you give it when signing up | giving the plan of the invitation |
| IP address, browser, session time | automatically when signing in | security, preventing abuse, sign-in sessions |
| Two-step sign-in data (2FA), if you turn it on | generated in the app | securing the account |

**We do not collect or store card details.** You give them directly to the payment operator, which only tells us the status of the payment. We do not connect to your broker account: trade data comes only from you.

## 3. Legal bases

- **Performing the contract** (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, journal, statistics, subscription, technical e-mails (activation, password reset).
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): security, preventing abuse, pursuing claims, the “Week ahead”, “Day summary” and “What's new” e-mails (you can turn them off at any time in Settings → E-mails or with the link in the e-mail).
- **Legal obligation** (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): keeping data required by tax and accounting rules.

## 4. Who receives your data

Data is processed only by those needed to run the service:

- **Hosting:** our own server in Poland.
- **Stripe Payments Europe, Limited** (Ireland): payments and subscriptions. Stripe also acts as an independent controller of payment data.
- **Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA): carrying traffic and protecting the site.
- **Resend** (Plus Five Five, Inc., USA): sending e-mails.
- **Apple (iCloud+)**: the contact@ and support@ mailboxes, if you write to us.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on a European Commission decision (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

We fetch market data and the calendar from outside providers on the server side. We do not pass your personal data to them.

## 5. How long we keep data

- Account and journal data: until the account is deleted. When it is deleted, we delete them along with the screenshots.
- Payment data: for the period required by tax and accounting rules, usually 5 years.
- Database backups: up to 30 days from the backup.
- Session data and security logs: up to 30 days.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, move it, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and complain to the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw).

You can export trades to CSV in the app. For anything else, write to contact@alphalogfx.com.

## 7. Cookies and browser storage

We use only what is necessary:

- **session cookie**: keeps you signed in (up to 30 days);
- **demo cookie**: keeps the demo open (up to 12 hours);
- **browser storage (localStorage)**: remembers your choices, such as theme, language, the folded menu and chart settings.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. The payment page belongs to Stripe and follows its rules.

## 8. Security

We store passwords only as cryptographic hashes, connections are encrypted (HTTPS), only a trade's owner can see its screenshots, and sign-in can be secured with a second step (2FA).

## 9. Changes to this policy

We announce material changes by e-mail or in the app at least 14 days before they take effect.
